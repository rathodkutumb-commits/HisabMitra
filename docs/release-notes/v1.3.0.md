### HisabMitra 1.3.0 — Family hisab (પરિવારનો હિસાબ)

**One hisab for the whole home**
- Create a Family hisab (for example "Home"), with a starting balance and
  the people who share the home. Add people by name and mobile number —
  they don't need the app or an account.
- Record money in (salary, extra income, contribution) and money out
  (expenses with a category and who paid). Nothing is split.
- Every month reads like a passbook: opening balance, money in, money out
  and closing balance, carried into the next month.
- A monthly statement shows money in by person, money out by category and
  each person's part. Share it as a PDF (Gujarati prints correctly) or send
  the summary on WhatsApp to anyone with a number.
- The Home screen's income, expense, savings and chart include your Family
  hisab, with one line that says how much came from it.

**Share it with family who have the app**
- Invite a person with a "Tap to join" link. When they join, their account
  takes over their name and their earlier entries stay theirs.
- The owner and admins manage the Family hisab and its people; members add
  entries. Changes appear on everyone's phone within seconds.
- The same Join screen (and the same link) works for groups and Family
  hisab.

**Bills, assistant and reminders**
- Add bill photos or PDFs to money out; every member can open them.
- Ask the assistant: "How much did we spend on groceries at Home this
  month?", "What did Rahul put in?", "What is the Home balance?"
- A reminder can belong to a Family hisab (for example the rent on the
  5th). Recording its payment adds it to the Family hisab, and tapping the
  notification opens it.

**Categories in English and Gujarati**
- When you add a category, give it a name in both languages; the app shows
  the one for the language you use.
