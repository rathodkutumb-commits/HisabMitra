### First release of HisabMitra (હિસાબમિત્ર) — Smart Hisab, Happy Family

- Income and expenses with categories, search, filters, an Excel-like sheet
  with running balance, and CSV / PDF export (Gujarati-safe).
- Khata: money given and taken, partial repayments and due dates.
- Groups for family, trips and events: equal, exact, percentage, ratio and
  adjusted splits, balances, settle-up suggestions and history.
- Reports and charts, bills and receipts, reminders and recurring payments,
  encrypted backup and restore, calculator, optional AI assistant.
- English and Gujarati, with a one-page Gujarati keypad (barakhadi rows,
  half letters) and five Gujarati colour themes.
- Account verified by email; personal records stay on your phone.
- Guided tour with sample data on first launch.

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on your Android phone
(allow "install unknown apps" when asked). Later versions are offered
inside the app (More → About → Check for updates).
