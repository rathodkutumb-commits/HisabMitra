### HisabMitra 1.3.1 — small fixes for Family hisab

- **Statement:** if a Family hisab is deleted (here or on another phone)
  while its statement is open, the page now says so instead of loading
  forever.
- **Bills:** deleting a money-out entry also removes its bill photos from
  every member's phone.
- **Assistant in Gujarati:** questions with everyday word endings are
  understood — "આ મહિને ઘરમાં કરિયાણા પર કેટલો ખર્ચ થયો?" finds
  Groceries just like "કરિયાણું".
- **Home:** the quick actions are now two tidy rows of four. The calculator
  is still one tap away in the top bar (and in More).
