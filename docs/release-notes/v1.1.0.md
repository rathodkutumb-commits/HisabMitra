### HisabMitra 1.1.0 — your hisab saved to your account, and fixes

**Your hisab is now saved to your account**
- Entries, people, khata (money given and taken), repayments, reminders and
  bill photos are saved to your HisabMitra account. Only you can see them.
- The app still works without internet: changes made offline are saved to
  your account when you are back online.
- On a new or reset phone, sign in and everything comes back.
- After this update the app saves what is already on your phone to your
  account once ("Saving your hisab to your account"). You can keep using
  the app while it finishes.
- Every group is now saved to your account, so there is no "Share to
  cloud" step any more. Bill photos on group expenses are shared with the
  group automatically.
- Logging out removes the hisab from this phone (it stays in your account).
  Categories and settings stay on the phone.
- Automatic backups are removed. You can still make a backup file by hand;
  restoring one replaces the hisab saved in your account (groups are not
  changed).

**Fixes and improvements**
- Adding a bill photo from the camera or gallery no longer closes the app.
- The back button no longer closes the app by surprise: other tabs go back
  to Home, and on Home the app asks "Exit HisabMitra?".
- Reminders arrive at the time you set. A tip on the Reminders screen
  explains the battery setting some phones need.
- A floating AI button on the main pages opens the assistant, with more
  example questions grouped by topic.
- Group invitations and "Share HisabMitra" messages look cleaner in
  WhatsApp.
